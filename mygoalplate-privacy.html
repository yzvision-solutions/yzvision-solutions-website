MyGoalPlate Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 August 2026

This policy explains what MyGoalPlate collects, why, who it is shared with, how long it is kept, and how to get rid of it. It covers the MyGoalPlate mobile app and the MyGoalPlate web app.

1. What we collect

Account information

If you sign in with Google we receive your name, email address and profile picture. If you sign in with an email link we receive your email address. You can also use MyGoalPlate as a guest, in which case we hold no identifying information about you at all — only an anonymous identifier.

Health and goal information

The details you enter to calculate your targets: weight, height, age, gender and activity level; and the things you log: goals, meals, nutrition, and physical activity.

Meal photographs

Photographs you take or upload to have a meal analysed, and the nutrition estimates produced from them.

App settings

Preferences such as units, language, notification settings, and a record of which version of the terms you accepted.

Purchase and usage information

Your subscription status and plan, your credit balance, and a record of the AI features you have used. We do not receive or store your card details — see section 3.

Technical information

Standard server logs, and app performance measurements collected through Firebase Performance Monitoring.

2. Why we use it

  • To provide the service: calculating your targets, analysing meals, and keeping your log in sync across devices.
  • To operate subscriptions and credits, and to prevent the same purchase being redeemed more than once.
  • To protect the service from abuse, including rate limiting and verifying that requests come from a genuine copy of the app.
  • To diagnose faults and improve performance.

We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not sell it.

We never see your card details. Payments are handled entirely by Paddle or Google Play, who keep their own records under their own privacy policies and retention periods.

4. Where your information is stored

MyGoalPlate runs on Google Cloud infrastructure located in the United States. If you use the app from outside the United States, your information is transferred there.

6. Deleting your account

In the app: Settings → Delete Account. Deletion is immediate and permanent.

Can’t reach the app? Email info@yzvision-solutions.com from your account address.

This removes your sign-in, profile, goals, logged meals, meal photographs, settings, credit balance and usage history. Full detail, including the small amount of anonymised information we keep and why, is on the account deletion page.

If you have an active subscription, cancel it before deleting your account. We cannot cancel a Google Play subscription on your behalf, and deleting your account first would leave you being charged with no way to manage it.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access a copy of your information, correct it, delete it, object to or restrict how we use it, and receive it in a portable form. Most of this is available directly in the app: your profile and logged data are visible and editable in Settings and your goal history, and deletion is a single action.

For anything else, email info@yzvision-solutions.com. We aim to respond within 30 days. If you are in the UK or EU and are unhappy with our response, you may complain to your local data protection authority.

8. Children

MyGoalPlate is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Security

Access to your data is restricted so that only you can read it, and only our servers can change the parts that govern your subscription and credits. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest by our infrastructure provider. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Changes to this policy

If we make a significant change we will update the date at the top and, where the change materially affects you, ask you to review it in the app.

11. Contact

Questions about this policy or your information: info@yzvision-solutions.com.
